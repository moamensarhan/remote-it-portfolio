Case Study: User Forgot Email Credentials

---

Overview

User reported need to access their mail client, but forgot their password, and also their email. Secondary methods were used to restore account

Time to Resolution ~10 minutes

---

Environment

OS: Windows 10

E-mail Client: Gmail

---

Reported Symptoms

Inability to access crucial work information via email client

---

Initial Assessment

Verified that user forgot their email as well as their password

---

Investigation

Credential loss was already confirmed

User needed quick access to work material 

Phone number recovery was the fastest available option

---

Root Cause

Human forgetfulness combined with a lack of preparation (credentials were not stored)

---

Resolution

Through the associated phone number method provided by Google, user email was restored via SMS

That email was then used to recover user password through an OTP

New password was chosen by user

---

Verification

User regained access to email client and work resources

---

Advisory Note

Recommended digital storing of credentials over physical notes, as the latter is more prone to misplacement
